Roadie Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

What is this Privacy Policy and what does it cover?

The NicheNeeds Privacy Policy applies to everything NicheNeeds makes, including Roadie. This Roadie Privacy Policy adds the details that are specific to Roadie. If the two conflict, this one takes precedence.

The waitlist

When you join the waitlist we keep your email address. We use it only to tell you about Roadie, such as when a spot opens. We don't share it, and we'll delete it whenever you ask.

This page

The Roadie page sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Our host, Vercel, processes your IP address to deliver the page. The page loads its font from Google Fonts, so Google also receives your IP address.

Your content in Roadie

Roadie is invite-only. If you use it, you send it text, voice notes, photos and videos in a chat. Roadie turns them into posts: it transcribes what you say, adds subtitles to your video, writes a hook and prepares a caption from your own words.

We keep what you send and what Roadie makes from it, so that it can post for you and keep a record of your updates.

Connecting Roadie to apps and services

To do its job, Roadie passes your content to other services: Telegram for the chat, Anthropic's Claude to write the hook and caption, Vercel to host your media, Buffer to publish to your social channels, and Discord to post to your community. Each has its own terms and privacy policy.

Transcription happens on our own computer, not with a third party.

Media you send for posting is stored at a public link, because the platforms need to fetch it. Don't send Roadie anything you wouldn't post.

Roadie and ads

Roadie has no ads. We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Improving AI models

We don't use your content to train AI models. The AI services Roadie relies on handle data under their own terms.

Deleting Roadie data

Email us and we'll delete your waitlist entry, your content and your record of updates. Posts that are already published stay on those platforms until you delete them there.

Your rights

We're based in Amsterdam and follow the GDPR. You can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you. You can also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
